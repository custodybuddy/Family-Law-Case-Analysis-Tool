CustodyBuddy.com’s Family Law Case Analysis Tool offers a structured, step-by-step assistant for reviewing and strategizing family law cases. Its core features include:

1. **Case File Summarization** – Automatically summarizes uploaded legal documents, identifying parties, facts, issues, procedural posture, and key filings.

2. **Legal Issue Identification & Branching** – Detects primary legal issue types such as custody, support, property division, or domestic violence, and tailors all subsequent steps accordingly.

3. **Guided Legal Analysis (Steps 1–10)** – Breaks down each case into clear legal components:

   * Issue subtopics
   * Jurisdiction-specific statutes and rules
   * Definitions of key terms
   * Relevant legal trends
   * Arguments and counterarguments
   * Outcome evaluation
   * Strategy recommendations

4. **Interactive Court Form Automation (Step 11)** – Prepares jurisdiction-specific legal forms using a guided Q&A, with downloadable court-ready formats.

5. **Court Rule & Deadline Detection (Step 12)** – Identifies applicable local court rules and calculates filing deadlines, offering calendar integration.

6. **Automated Court Reminders (Step 13)** – Generates calendar events and email reminders for hearings and filing dates.

7. **Legal Aid Matching (Step 14)** – Suggests local legal aid services based on case type, jurisdiction, and optional income information.

8. **Document Comparison for Disputes (Step 15)** – Highlights contradictions between party declarations and suggests rebuttal or cross-examination content.

**Advanced Tools Include:**

* **Custody Schedule Builder** – Visualizes parenting time plans with court-compatible language.
* **Tone Analyzer** – Reviews declarations for bias or emotional tone, offering neutral rewording.
* **Live Rule Checker** – Uses web tools to retrieve the latest local family court rules and requirements.

This tool is designed to support litigants and legal professionals in navigating complex family law matters more efficiently and effectively.
